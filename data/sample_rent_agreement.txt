RENT AGREEMENT

This Rent Agreement is made on 1st November 2026 at Amravati between Mr. Ramesh Patil, hereinafter called the Lessor (Landlord), and Ms. Anita Sharma, hereinafter called the Lessee (Tenant).

WHEREAS the Lessor is the owner of Flat No. 12, Shivaji Nagar, Amravati and has agreed to let the said flat to the Lessee for residential use only.

1. TERM. The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 months commencing from 1st November 2026. This agreement shall be deemed to be renewed automatically for a further period of 11 months unless the Lessee gives written notice of non-renewal.

2. RENT. The Lessee shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 12,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. The rent shall increase by 10% every year. For any delay in payment, interest at 3% per month shall be charged on the unpaid rent.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Lessee has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 60,000. The Lessor may deduct any amount at his sole discretion for repairs, painting or any other charges, and the balance shall be returned within 60 days after vacant possession is handed over.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD. The Lessee shall not vacate the flat during the first 6 months. If the Lessee leaves earlier, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited.

5. TERMINATION. The Lessor may terminate this agreement at any time without any prior notice if the Lessee violates any term, and the Lessee shall vacate the flat forthwith. The Lessee must give 2 months written notice to terminate.

6. SUB-LETTING. The Lessee shall not sub-let the flat or any part of it to any person.

7. MAINTENANCE. The Lessee shall pay electricity, water and society maintenance charges and shall keep the flat in good condition. The Lessee agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Lessor against all claims, losses and legal costs arising out of the Lessee's use of the flat.

8. DISPUTES. Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be referred to arbitration, and the courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement on the date mentioned above.
